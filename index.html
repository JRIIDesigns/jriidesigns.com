About

I'm Ben, and JRII Designs is what I do after my real job.

I design things I actually need: a holder for this, a shelf for that, a fix for whatever is annoying me that week. Once it works, I give it away. Every model here is free. So is PrintBinder. Nothing on this site costs anything, and nothing ever will.

I do it because making things is fun, and because this hobby only works when people share what they have figured out. This is my share.

Software

PrintBinder

A free Windows program for browsing and organising a 3D-printing library. It shows every model with a preview picture, opens models in 3D, and helps bring scattered files into one tidy library. It never moves, renames or removes anything you have not approved.
