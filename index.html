About

I'm Ben, and JRII Designs is what I do outside of work.

It started with 3D printing: things I actually need, like a holder for this, a shelf for that, a fix for whatever is annoying me that week. Once a model works, I give it away. Every model I publish is free, and so is PrintBinder.

It has grown from there. Now it is also software, a 3D walkthrough of real buildings, and tools I build because I want them to exist. I do it because making things is fun.

Much of it is AI assisted. Some of my models I drew entirely myself. Others, along with the software and the walkthrough, were made with an AI assistant helping me design, write and build. The ideas and the decisions are mine.

Projects

Three things I am working on in my own time right now, all built with AI assistance. Each one lives on its own; this page is the way in.

  • Free software

    PrintBinder

    A free Windows program for browsing and organising a 3D-printing library. It shows every model with a preview picture, opens models in 3D, and never moves, renames or removes anything you have not approved.

    New 2.2.3: a built-in browser

    See PrintBinder

  • 3D walkthrough

    Cobblestone on the Lake

    A 3D model of two buildings in Fort Myers, made from the architectural plans. Walk through the apartments, townhomes, garages and stairs in your browser, on a computer or a phone.

    Open the walkthrough

  • Private, in testing

    Rent Manager connector

    An in-house connector I developed that lets an AI assistant work with Rent Manager, the property management software. It is private and I am testing it now, so there is nothing to download.

    Not public yet.
